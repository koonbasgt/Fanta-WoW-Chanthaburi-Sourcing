# Fanta-WoW-Chanthaburi-Sourcing
​🥤 Fanta x WoW: Strategic Portfolio & Direct-Sourcing Analysis

​An executive-level financial and supply chain management case study for a Fanta x World of Warcraft (WoW) limited-time flavor campaign. This project models a direct-from-farm procurement hub in Chanthaburi, Thailand, demonstrating how local sourcing mitigates supply chain risks, slashes logistics costs, and drives high portfolio profit margins.

​📌 Executive Summary
​By leveraging Chanthaburi’s unique agricultural strength as a direct-sourcing "Lab Market," this campaign introduces a strategically balanced tri-SKU portfolio:
​Chanthaburi Gold Pineapple
(Core / High Volume Driver)
​Lychee-Lime Tropical
(Seasonal / Premium Offering)
​Durian-Banana "Regional Exclusive" (Limited Edition / Scarcity-Driven)
​By combining Direct Local Sourcing with Value-Based Pricing (leveraging high-value WoW digital in-game assets), the campaign achieves a Blended Gross Margin of 52.31%.

🌿 Inbound Supply Chain: Local Hub vs. Global Import
​The key competitive advantage lies in establishing a direct procurement hub in Chanthaburi compared to traditional global import models.


Key Supply Chain Insights:
​🌱 Raw Material COGS Savings: Purchasing directly from local Chanthaburi farms reduces raw fruit concentrate costs from $0.09 to $0.05 per unit (a 44.4% cost reduction).
​🚚 Inbound Logistics Efficiency: Localizing the supply chain slashes inbound freight costs from $0.05 to $0.01 per unit (an 80% saving in logistics).
​📉 Total Cost Advantage: Total inbound landed cost drops from $0.14 to $0.06 per unit, unlocking significant margin expansion across all SKUs.

​🕹️ Behavioral Economics & Portfolio Strategy
​The Durian-Banana "Wildcard" SKU
​Collector Pricing: Priced at a premium $2.50 SRP, backed by exclusive in-game World of Warcraft digital rewards.
​Economic Moat: Delivers a exceptional 63.40% Gross Margin, single-handedly elevating the blended campaign profitability.

​Agile Risk Management:
Restricted to a 10% sales mix as a regional exclusive, minimizing national market risk while maximizing brand buzz and profitability.

​🛠️ Data Model & Calculation Methodology
​Blended Profit per Unit: =SUMPRODUCT(Unit Profits, Sales Mix) \rightarrow $0.76
​Blended Gross Margin: =Blended Profit / Average SRP \rightarrow 52.31%
​Stress-Tested Margin: Evaluates portfolio resilience under a +20% surge in raw fruit prices, maintaining a robust 51.35% Gross Margin.
